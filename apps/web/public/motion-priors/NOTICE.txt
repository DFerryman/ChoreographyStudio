Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

Sources: CMU subject 85 trial 12 and subject 61 trial 08, via the previously
published retargeted motion-only portions of Choreography Studio's public
complex-street-dance and salsa-dance companion packs. No source audio,
scene project, editing history, or private uploaded file is included here.

CMU homepage: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
"This data is free for use in research projects. You may include this data
in commercially-sold products, but you may not resell this data directly,
even in converted form."

CMU FAQ: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php
"The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or redistributed
without permission."

BVH converter: Bruce Hahne, MotionBuilder-friendly BVH conversion 2010.
The converter adds no restrictions to the original CMU data.
https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap/blob/master/READMEFIRST.txt

These derivatives are included as internal motion priors in the editor,
not offered as a standalone commercial motion-data resale product.
CMU does not guarantee the quality of the original motion data.

Processing: canonical neutral-rig-2 retarget; original published source
anomaly repair and support-height adaptation; 30 Hz plus exact end time;
float32 coordinates/quaternions; fixed +/-30-degree forearm carrying
frame redistributed into upper-arm axial twist; wrist longitudinal-Y
pronation reassigned to forearm within +/-110 degrees. The carrying-frame
change preserves forearm/hand world transforms. Pronation redistribution
then changes forearm axial orientation while preserving hand world
position/orientation and hand-tip transforms. These mathematical frame
changes are not a physical feasibility certificate.

Ankle longitudinal-Y excess is smoothly transferred to tibial axial-Y
within the existing +/-25-degree tibial budget, toward the unchanged
+/-30-degree ankle envelope. The paired inverse rotations preserve foot,
toe and heel world transforms while changing shin skin axial orientation.
Residual excess in advanced floor-motion frames remains for the physical
gate; this is engineering retargeting with knee-flexion dependence, not
clinical range certification or a guarantee every reference is feasible.

New generated trajectories may further adapt arm targets to the pinned
avatar's body/hand geometry and condition noisy arm rotation curves.
Those changes are versioned and their measured hand-path displacement
is retained in generation provenance; original scene and source motion
files are never changed by generation or playback.

CMU61 Salsa new-candidate stance calibration also reduces donor hip axial
twist and adds a measured outward stance to prevent actual target-avatar
thigh skin crossings. Source timing/knee hinge motion is retained; ankle
paths and sole orientation are explicit soft goals with measured maximum
and RMS displacement recorded in provenance. This runtime retargeting does
not modify these static source derivatives or waive anatomy/collision gates.

Build and provenance details: docs/MOTION_PRIORS.md and
